Calculation flow (Manila current month only)
- Positive records add points; Negative records subtract points.
- 90+ net points: 2 Stars / Excellent
- 70-89 net points: 1 Star / Good
- 0-69 net points: No Award
- Below 0 net points: 1 Demerit / Needs Improvement
- No current-month records: Not Yet Evaluated
- Results recalculate on model save/edit/delete and after AJAX submission.
- Stars are capped at 2 and demerits at 1.

Monthly reset
- Render cron runs at 16:05 UTC on day 1 (00:05 Asia/Manila).
- The command checks Manila date and uses MonthlyCleanupRun for idempotency.
- Before deletion, one MonthlyPerformanceSummary per employee/month is saved.
- Previous-month PerformanceRecord rows are deleted.
- Employee monthly points/stars/demerits/status reset to defaults.
- Employee names, positions, and photos are preserved.

Deployment
1. Replace files using the included paths.
2. Run: python manage.py migrate
3. Push render.yaml and ensure the Render cron service is deployed.
4. The Django migration applies the schema directly to the configured Supabase PostgreSQL database; separate SQL is not required.
